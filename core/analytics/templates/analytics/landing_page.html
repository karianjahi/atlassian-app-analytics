Atlassian App Analytics

This mini-project is a customer behaviour analytics prototype for Atlassian Marketplace app vendors.

Background

Atlassian Marketplace vendors build cloud apps that support teams using Jira and Confluence. As these apps grow, product teams need better ways to understand customer usage, adoption, reliability issues, and churn risk.

Motivation

The goal of this project is to turn product usage events into practical insights. Instead of only storing raw customer activity, the system calculates customer health scores, highlights risk signals, and recommends actions for product or support teams.

Inspired by codefortynine

codefortynine GmbH builds Atlassian cloud apps for Jira and Confluence. Their app portfolio includes products such as Deep Clone for Jira, External Data for Jira Fields, Quick Filters for Jira Dashboards, Merge Agent for Jira, and Snipe-IT for Jira. Their tools focus on saving time, improving workflows, connecting external data, and extending Jira dashboards.

What this prototype does

  • Tracks customer usage events
  • Calculates customer health scores
  • Detects churn-risk signals
  • Visualizes feature usage and activity over time
  • Suggests recommended actions for customer success
