Customer Intelligence for Marketplace Apps

Turn Atlassian app usage data into customer health, churn risk, and actionable insights.

Atlassian App Analytics helps Marketplace-style app vendors understand product adoption, detect customer risk, forecast health, and prioritize intervention using analytics and machine learning.

Upload CSV Data Explore Dashboard

Upload your own customer event data or explore the dashboard using demo data.

What the platform does

  • Ingests customer event data
  • Calculates health scores
  • Predicts churn probability
  • Explains risk drivers
  • Generates alerts and forecasts

Workflow

From raw usage events to customer intelligence

The application converts uploaded customer activity into useful analytics that product and customer success teams can act on.

01

Upload data

Upload a CSV file containing customers, app names, installation dates, event types, timestamps, and metadata.

02

Process events

The system validates the file, stores customer records, skips duplicate events, and logs the upload.

03

Refresh analytics

Customer health scores, churn risk, ML predictions, alerts, insights, and forecasts are refreshed automatically.

04

Explore insights

Use the dashboard and customer detail pages to identify risk, understand trends, and prioritize action.

Capabilities

A full-stack analytics platform

This project combines backend engineering, REST APIs, frontend rendering, data science, machine learning, and deployment into one complete product.

CSV ingestion Validated uploads with duplicate protection.
Health scoring Rule-based scoring from usage, adoption, reliability, and support data.
ML churn prediction Logistic regression model with feature importance.
Historical tracking Snapshots, trends, insights, alerts, and forecasts.
